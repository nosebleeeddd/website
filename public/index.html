About Me

I’m a cybersecurity enthusiast and hands-on technologist who likes understanding how things work at every layer — from software and networks down to firmware, electronics, and RF.

My background is primarily in Linux, cybersecurity, networking, embedded systems, and hardware. I’ve worked with penetration testing and CTFs, SOC-related security, PowerShell, Python, C/C++, ESP32 development, custom electronics, radio systems, and network infrastructure.

A lot of my projects start with a simple question: “What happens if I build it myself?” That has led me into firmware development, PCB design, soldering, RF experimentation, custom cybersecurity hardware, and Linux-based tooling.

I document the things I build, break, learn, and figure out along the way.
