# Return & Refund Policy

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

Thank you for choosing **Nosebleeeddd Customs**. Because we sell custom-built electronics and provide repair, modification, and soldering services, our return policy depends on the type of order.

## Physical Products

Unused, non-modified products may be eligible for a return within **30 days of delivery**.

To be eligible for a return:

* The item must be unused and in its original condition.
* The item must include all original components and accessories.
* The item must not have been modified, disassembled, damaged, or altered after delivery.
* Proof of purchase may be required.

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item was defective, damaged before delivery, or the return was otherwise approved by Nosebleeeddd Customs.

Once the returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund will be issued to the original payment method.

## Custom-Built Devices

Custom-built devices are made specifically according to the customer's requested specifications and are **generally non-returnable once work has begun**.

Before work begins, Nosebleeeddd Customs will confirm the requested specifications and agreed price with the customer.

If a custom device arrives damaged or has a manufacturing defect, please contact Nosebleeeddd Customs as soon as possible so the issue can be reviewed.

## Repairs

Repair services are generally **non-refundable once repair work has been performed**.

If a repaired device experiences an issue related to the work performed, contact Nosebleeeddd Customs so the issue can be reviewed.

Repair outcomes can depend on the condition, age, previous modifications, and underlying damage of the device. Additional problems unrelated to the original repair may require a separate quote.

## Modifications

Modification work is generally **non-refundable once the modification has been performed**.

Customers are responsible for confirming that requested modifications are suitable for their intended use before work begins.

If a modification does not function as agreed, contact Nosebleeeddd Customs so the issue can be evaluated.

## Customer-Supplied Hardware

For repairs and modifications involving customer-supplied hardware, Nosebleeeddd Customs is not responsible for pre-existing defects, hidden damage, incompatible components, or failures unrelated to the requested work.

Customers should back up any important data before sending a device for service.

## Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, contact Nosebleeeddd Customs as soon as possible and provide photographs of the packaging and damaged item.

Do not discard the original packaging until the shipping issue has been reviewed.

## Cancellations

Orders for physical products that have not yet shipped may be eligible for cancellation.

Custom builds, repairs, and modifications may not be cancellable once work or parts procurement has begun.

## Contact

For return, refund, or service-related questions, contact Nosebleeeddd Customs through the contact information provided on this website.

Each return or refund request will be reviewed based on the type and condition of the order.

**Nosebleeeddd Customs reserves the right to evaluate return and refund requests on a case-by-case basis.**
